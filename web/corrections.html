Report a problem

Anything the site says about a person or a group can be reported, not only briefings: a member's role or office on the day, their majority and its band, the party that came second in their seat, a connection to a party group (including payments recorded by IPSA), a registered interest flagged beside what they said, a grouping by age, gender or seat, or the summary on a member's page. For a briefing, the quickest way is the "Report a problem" link under it, which tells us which briefing you mean. You can also use this form, or email admin@wherempsstand.com.

Briefings are written by AI from the official record. Where something is wrong, it is corrected, or the briefing withdrawn, and listed above. The record itself is Parliament's: to correct Hansard, contact the Hansard editors.
