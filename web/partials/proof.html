How the briefings are checked
  • The AI is given only the Hansard and other parliamentary records found for your question, and may use nothing else. Early day motions, committee evidence and the devolved parliaments' reports are given too, when they bear on the question.
  • Each point links to its source. Citations are checked against the source text, quotations are checked word for word, and sentences that no source supports are flagged.
  • Hansard is read in twice a day.
  • If the record holds nothing relevant, the site says “No records found”.
  • Evidence is drawn from across ministers, the opposition and backbenchers.
